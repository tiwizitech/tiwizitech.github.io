What is Tiwizi?

Strength comes from cooperation.

Tiwizi is a traditional Amazigh principle of collective solidarity and mutual aid. It refers to a form of voluntary cooperation where members of a community work together to help one another, without financial compensation.

It is based on a simple idea: a community is strong when its members support each other.

How Tiwizi works

When someone needs help, the community comes together.

In Amazigh society, Tiwizi has always mobilized everyone around the moments that matter most.

🌾

Harvesting crops

Neighbours gather to bring in the harvest, field after field.

🏡

Building a house

Hands and skills come together to raise a home for a family.

💧

Farming & irrigation

Shared work on land and water that sustains the whole village.

🎉

Weddings & communal events

Everyone helps prepare the celebrations that unite the community.

✕ No money is exchanged ✦ The reward is solidarity, dignity, and shared responsibility
